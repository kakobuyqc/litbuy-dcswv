Litbuy shopping guide

From an interesting link to an informed choice.

Good product discovery ends with a few specific checks. Keep your shortlist small enough to compare the details that matter.

Build your shortlist ↗

  1. Choose a category and a purpose

    Start with the item you need: an everyday T-shirt, a layering hoodie, a jersey or a pair of shoes. Record your preferred fit and intended use.

  2. Read the listing beyond the title

    Check the selected variant, material description, dimensions and seller information. Treat unclear or missing details as questions to resolve.

  3. Compare measurements and photos

    Use a garment you own as a sizing reference. If inspection photos are available, check the visible color, construction, labels and selected variant.

  4. Confirm the estimated total

    Account for applicable delivery charges, service fees, conversion costs, taxes and duties. Review current return conditions before paying.

A reusable comparison framework

What belongs in your product notes?

These fields make a clothing or shoes spreadsheet more useful than a long collection of unexplained links.

Product comparison checklist
Field What to record Why it helps
Product and variant Listing title, product link, color and selected size. Keeps comparisons tied to the exact option you intend to buy.
Measurements Relevant garment dimensions or the listing's shoe size chart. Provides a stronger fit reference than a size label alone.
Materials and construction The seller's stated composition, fastening and care details. Highlights missing information and differences between listings.
Photo observations Visible color, shape, stitching and any available inspection images. Helps identify visible mismatches without assuming unseen quality.
Estimated total cost Item price plus applicable fees, delivery and import charges. Makes budget comparisons more realistic.
Seller and return terms Seller identity, published policies and the date checked. Preserves the conditions you reviewed before ordering.

No prices, delivery promises or seller ratings are invented on this page. Check the current product and checkout information for your destination.

Quick answers

Before you follow the next product link.

Direct answers to common questions about spreadsheet lists, sizing, QC photos and shopping budgets.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

In shopping searches, a Litbuy spreadsheet generally means a collected list of product links and comparison notes. Its usefulness depends on the accuracy of each listing, not the number of links it contains.

Is this an official Litbuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery and shopping guide. Its browsing links lead to CAFANS, and it does not claim affiliation with Litbuy or the fashion brands mentioned.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the listing's garment measurements with a similar item you already own. Check chest width, length, shoulder width, waist and inseam where relevant, and confirm how the seller measures each dimension.

Can a spreadsheet prove that a branded item is authentic?

No. A spreadsheet entry, logo, product photo or QC image does not establish authenticity. Check the seller's identity, supporting documentation and applicable return terms.

What should I include in an estimated shopping budget?

Include the item price and any applicable domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping, currency conversion, taxes and duties. Confirm current charges before payment.

Does this page contain a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides category browsing links and a comparison checklist. It does not provide a downloadable spreadsheet or a verified live inventory feed.
